Goodquestions

Do I need a permit for a kitchen or bathroom remodel in Marina Del Rey?

Often, yes. Moving plumbing, adding circuits or changing walls usually needs one. Marina Del Rey is unincorporated Los Angeles County, so permits go through LA County Public Works Building & Safety, not the City of LA. After the site visit we'll tell you what your project needs.

I live in a condo. What does the HOA need?

Every building is different, but expect an approval letter for the scope, a certificate of insurance naming the association, elevator and parking reservations, protection for hallways, and set work hours. Send us your building's remodel rules and we'll plan around them.

How long does a remodel take?

It depends on scope, material lead times and inspections. A cosmetic refresh moves faster than a layout change. You'll get a schedule once the scope is set, and one Google reviewer noted we "finished the job on time."

What will it cost?

We don't quote from a web form. Price depends on size, finishes, and what's behind the walls. After a site visit we'll walk you through the cost before anything is ordered.

Can I stay home during the work?

For a bathroom, usually yes if you have a second one. For a kitchen, most people set up a temporary station with a microwave and a fridge in another room. We'll talk through it before demo day.

Do you only do kitchens and bathrooms?

They're the focus. Customers have also reviewed us for a living room remodel, new flooring, lighting and finished walls. If you have something else in mind, ask.
